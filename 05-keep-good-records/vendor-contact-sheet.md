# 📇 Vendor Contact Sheet

**A single place to keep important phone numbers, account details, and support contacts.**

---

## Why This Matters

When something breaks — your internet goes down, your email stops working, or you get hacked — you don't want to spend an hour hunting for who to call. This sheet puts every vendor contact in one place.

**Time to complete:** 15-30 minutes the first time · **Cost:** $0 · **Update:** annually, or whenever a vendor changes

---

## Security Warning

> ⚠️ **Store this sheet securely.** It contains account numbers and support information that could help an attacker impersonate you. Keep it in a restricted Google Drive folder or a locked cabinet — not a public link. Never post it on your public website.

## What to Include for Each Vendor

- **What they provide** — one line so anyone can tell what this vendor is for
- **Account number / ID** — helps support find you fast (store the password separately in Bitwarden)
- **Support phone** — the number you call when something breaks
- **Support email or portal** — alternative contact method
- **Admin login** — note that the login is stored in Bitwarden (never write the actual password here)
- **Notes** — billing cycle, renewal date, dedicated rep, anything else useful
