# 📝 Keep Good Records

**Documentation and templates for organized, secure IT operations.**

---

## Why This Matters

**Good records help you:**
- Know what technology you have and where it is
- Respond quickly to security incidents
- Train new volunteers efficiently
- Pass audits and grant requirements
- Recover from disasters faster

**Poor records lead to:**
- Lost passwords and locked accounts
- Forgotten software subscriptions wasting money
- Missing equipment you paid for
- Chaos when someone leaves
- Failed audits

---

- ✅ Create an asset inventory

- ✅ Track passwords and vendor contacts securely
- ✅ Have compliance-ready documentation

## The Essential Documents

Every non-profit should have these:

### 1. Asset Inventory
**What:** List of all technology equipment  
**Why:** Know what you have, who has it, warranty status  
**Update:** When equipment is bought, moved, or retired

### 2. Account & Password Inventory
**What:** List of all online accounts and services  
**Why:** Never lose access, cancel unused subscriptions  
**Stored in:** Bitwarden (secure), not a spreadsheet

### 3. Vendor Contact List
**What:** Important phone numbers and support contacts  
**Why:** Quick help when things break  
**Update:** Annually or when vendors change

### 4. Security Policies
**What:** Written rules for technology use  
**Why:** Set expectations, compliance, training reference  
**Include:** Password policy, acceptable use, remote work rules

### 5. Incident Response Plan
**What:** What to do when security incidents happen  
**Why:** Respond quickly and correctly  
**Cover:** Hacked accounts, lost devices, data breaches

### 6. Privacy & Compliance Documentation
**What:** Data protection policies and procedures  
**Why:** Meet GDPR/CCPA requirements, avoid fines  
**Include:** Privacy notice, data retention schedule, DPA templates

### 7. Security Monitoring Procedures
**What:** How to review logs and detect threats  
**Why:** Early breach detection, compliance requirements  
**Include:** Log review schedule, alert procedures, investigation steps

### 8. Physical Security Plan
**What:** Office security measures and procedures  
**Why:** Protect equipment and paper records  
**Include:** Access control, visitor procedures, emergency plan

## Quick Start: 1-Hour Documentation Sprint

If you only have 1 hour, do this:

**0-15 min:** List all computers and phones
- Make: Model: Serial #: Who has it

**15-30 min:** List all online accounts
- Service name: Admin email: Purpose

**30-45 min:** List important vendor contacts
- Internet provider: Account #: Support phone

**45-60 min:** Write down one emergency contact
- Who to call if you get hacked
