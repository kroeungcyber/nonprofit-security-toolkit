# 📦 Asset Tracking Template

**A simple inventory of your organization's computers, phones, and other equipment.**

---

## Why This Matters

If you don't know what equipment you have, who has it, and where it is, you can't protect it. An asset inventory helps you:

- Find lost or stolen devices quickly (serial numbers are essential for police reports)
- Know what needs security updates and maintenance
- Track who is responsible for each device
- Pass audits and grant requirements
- Recover equipment when volunteers leave

**Time to create:** 30 minutes initially, then update when things change

---

## How to Use This Template

Copy the tables below into your own spreadsheet or document (Google Sheets works well and is free). Fill in one row per device. Update it whenever equipment is bought, moved, assigned, or retired.

**Store it securely** — this list contains serial numbers and who has each device, so don't leave it in a shared, public location.
