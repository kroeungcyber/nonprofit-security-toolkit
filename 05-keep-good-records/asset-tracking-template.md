# 📋 Asset Tracking Template

**A simple inventory of your computers, phones, and other equipment.**

---

## Why This Matters

If you don't know what equipment you have, who has it, and where it is, you can't:
- Know what to protect
- Recover equipment when someone leaves
- Track warranty and purchase dates
- Pass an audit

**Time to complete:** 30-60 minutes the first time · **Cost:** $0 · **Update:** whenever equipment is bought, moved, or retired

---

## How to Use This Template

Copy the table below into a spreadsheet (Google Sheets is free) or keep it as a document. Fill in one row per item.

**Tip:** Google Sheets is the easiest option — the whole team can view and update it, and it's backed up automatically.

### Column Definitions

| Column | What to Write |
|--------|---------------|
| **Asset ID** | A short unique label (001, 002, ...) you can write on a sticker |
| **Type** | Laptop, desktop, phone, tablet, monitor, printer, external drive, etc. |
| **Make / Model** | Brand and model, so you can look up support or parts |
| **Serial Number** | Found on a sticker on the device or in its settings |
| **Assigned To** | The person's name, or "Office", "Storage", or "Unassigned" |
| **Status** | In use, Storage, Needs repair, Retired, Lost/Stolen |
| **Purchase Date** | When it was bought (approximate is fine) |
| **Warranty Ends** | When the warranty expires (or "None") |
| **Notes** | Anything useful — damage, accessories, login details location |

---

## Quick Reference: Where to Find the Serial Number

- **Windows laptop:** Type "system information" in the Start menu, or check the sticker on the bottom
- **Mac:** Click the Apple menu > About This Mac
- **iPhone/iPad:** Settings > General > About
- **Android:** Settings > About Phone
- **Printer / monitor:** Check the sticker on the back or bottom

## Disposal Checklist

When equipment is retired, do this **before** disposal or donation:

- [ ] Back up any needed files
- [ ] Wipe the device (see the [Device Security guide](../03-protect-your-devices/windows-laptop-security.md) for wiping steps)
- [ ] Remove from this inventory (mark as "Retired" with the date)
- [ ] Recycle or donate responsibly — see your local electronics recycling program
