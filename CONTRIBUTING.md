# Contributing to NonProfit Security Toolkit

Thank you for your interest in helping non-profits stay secure! This project thrives on contributions from people who've helped resource-constrained organizations navigate technology challenges.

---

## How to Contribute

### 1. Share Your Experience

Have you helped a non-profit with their IT security? We'd love to hear:
- What worked well
- What challenges you faced
- Lessons learned
- Tools and techniques that were especially helpful

**How to share:**
- Open an issue with the label `experience-report`
- Or email your story (if you prefer to remain anonymous)

### 2. Improve Documentation

Found something unclear? Have a better explanation?

**Areas where help is always welcome:**
- Simplifying technical concepts
- Adding screenshots or diagrams
- Translating to other languages
- Updating outdated information
- Fixing typos and broken links

**How to contribute:**
1. Fork the repository
2. Make your changes
3. Submit a pull request
4. Describe what you changed and why

### 3. Add New Content

**We'd love additions like:**
- Step-by-step guides for specific tools
- Case studies from real non-profits
- Templates and checklists
- Scripts for automation
- Training materials

**Guidelines for new content:**
- Keep it simple and jargon-free
- Focus on free or low-cost solutions
- Remember the audience: non-technical volunteers
- Include practical examples
- Test your instructions before submitting

### 4. Report Issues

Found something wrong or missing?

**Before reporting:**
- Check if the issue already exists
- Verify the problem (not just a one-time glitch)

**When reporting:**
- Be specific about what you found
- Include steps to reproduce
- Suggest a fix if you have one
- Be kind and constructive

## Content Guidelines

### Writing Style

**Do:**
- Write like you're explaining to a smart friend who's not technical
- Use analogies to explain complex concepts
- Include specific steps (click here, type this)
- Add time estimates and difficulty levels
- Provide context for why something matters

**Don't:**
- Use technical jargon without explaining it
- Assume prior knowledge
- Be vague ("configure your settings" — which settings?)
- Skip steps because they seem obvious

### Formatting

- Use clear headings
- Include tables for comparisons
- Use checklists for step-by-step procedures
- Bold important warnings
- Keep paragraphs short and scannable

### Tools and Recommendations

When suggesting tools:
- Prioritize free options
- Mention the cost if there is one
- Explain why you recommend this tool
- Note any limitations or requirements
- Include alternatives when possible

## Areas We Especially Need Help

### 1. Case Studies

Real-world examples of non-profits implementing these security measures:
- What was their situation before?
- What did they implement?
- What challenges did they face?
- What were the results?

### 2. Additional Platforms

Guides for:
- Linux workstations
- Chromebooks
- iPad/tablet deployments
- Other non-Windows/Mac environments

### 3. Industry-Specific Guidance

Security considerations for:
- Healthcare non-profits (HIPAA considerations)
- Educational organizations (FERPA)
- International NGOs (data sovereignty)
- Small grassroots organizations (extremely limited resources)

### 4. Translations

Making these materials accessible in other languages.

### 5. Visual Aids

- Diagrams of security concepts
- Screenshots of configuration steps
- Flowcharts for decision-making
- Infographics for volunteer training

## Code of Conduct

### Our Pledge

We are committed to providing a friendly, safe, and welcoming environment for all contributors.

### Our Standards

**Positive behavior:**
- Being respectful and inclusive
- Accepting constructive criticism gracefully
- Focusing on what's best for non-profits
- Showing empathy for users with limited technical resources

**Unacceptable behavior:**
- Trolling, insulting comments, or personal attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information
- Other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate

### Scope

This code applies within project spaces and when representing the project in public spaces.

### Enforcement

Violations may result in temporary or permanent ban from project spaces.
