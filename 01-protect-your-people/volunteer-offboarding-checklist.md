# 🚪 Volunteer Offboarding Checklist

**Securely remove access when volunteers leave your organization.**

---

## Why This Matters

When volunteers leave — whether they quit, finish their commitment, or are asked to step down — they often still have access to:
- Email accounts
- Donor databases
- Social media
- Cloud files
- Shared passwords

**This is a major security risk.** Former volunteers with lingering access can:
- Accidentally or maliciously delete important data
- Access donor information they shouldn't see
- Post on social media as your organization
- Create confusion about who is authorized to act on your behalf

**Real story:** A non-profit discovered a former volunteer still had access to their Facebook page a year after leaving. During a disagreement about a new policy, the former volunteer posted inflammatory content that damaged the organization's reputation.

---

## What You'll Accomplish

- ✅ Complete removal of all digital access
- ✅ Recovery of organization equipment
- ✅ Documentation of what was removed
- ✅ Clean handoff of ongoing work

**Time:** 30-60 minutes  
**Cost:** $0  
**When to do this:** Within 24 hours of someone leaving

---

## Before the Volunteer Leaves

### 1. Schedule an Exit Conversation (15 minutes)

**Have a conversation about:**
- Why they're leaving (if appropriate)
- What projects need to be handed off
- When their last day will be
- What access they currently have

**Ask them:**
- What accounts do you have access to?
- What passwords do you know?
- Do you have any organization files on personal devices?
- Are you logged into our accounts on any devices?

**Document their answers.** You may discover access you forgot about.

---

### 2. Plan the Handoff (10 minutes)

**Identify:**
- What ongoing work needs to be transferred
- Who will take over their responsibilities
- What files need to be moved to shared locations
- Any important contacts or relationships to document

---

## On the Last Day

### 3. Immediate Access Removal (15 minutes)

Do these **immediately** when they leave:

#### Email & Accounts

- [ ] **Disable Google Workspace/Email account**
  - Don't delete immediately (you might need to forward emails)
  - Suspend or disable the account
  - Set up email forwarding to their supervisor
  - After 30 days, you can delete or archive

- [ ] **Remove from all Google Groups and distribution lists**
  - Board announcements
  - Volunteer updates
  - Event planning groups

- [ ] **Remove from shared calendars**
  - Remove their access to organization calendars
  - Remove organization events from their personal calendar

#### Passwords & Shared Access

- [ ] **Remove from Bitwarden Organization**
  - Go to your Bitwarden Organization
  - Go to Manage > People
  - Find the person and click "Remove"
  - This immediately removes access to all shared passwords

- [ ] **Change passwords they knew personally**
  - Any shared passwords they had memorized
  - WiFi passwords (if they knew them)
  - Door codes or alarm codes

#### Social Media & Online Accounts

- [ ] **Remove as admin from Facebook page**
  - Page Settings > Page Roles > Remove person

- [ ] **Remove from Instagram business account**
  - Settings > Account > Multi-account login or linked accounts

- [ ] **Remove from Twitter/X account**
  - Settings > Team > Remove member

- [ ] **Remove from LinkedIn Company Page**
  - Admin tools > Manage admins > Remove

- [ ] **Remove from website admin**
  - WordPress: Users > find user > Delete
  - Squarespace: Settings > Permissions > Remove
  - Wix: Dashboard > Roles & Permissions > Remove

#### Software & Services

- [ ] **Remove from donor database/CRM**
  - Deactivate or delete their user account
  - Reassign any contacts/opportunities they own

- [ ] **Remove from project management tools**
  - Asana, Trello, Monday.com, etc.

- [ ] **Remove from accounting software**
  - QuickBooks, Xero, etc.

- [ ] **Remove from file storage**
  - Google Drive: Remove from shared drives
  - Dropbox: Remove from shared folders
  - OneDrive: Remove sharing permissions

- [ ] **Remove from video conferencing**
  - Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams accounts

#### Physical Access

- [ ] **Collect keys**
  - Office keys
  - Desk keys
  - File cabinet keys
  - Storage unit keys

- [ ] **Collect access cards/badges**
  - Building access cards
  - Parking passes

- [ ] **Change door codes if necessary**
  - If they knew alarm codes, change them
  - If they knew keypad codes, change them

---

### 4. Equipment Return (10 minutes)

**Collect and document:**

- [ ] **Laptop/Computer**
  - Record serial number
  - Check for damage
  - Verify it's functional

- [ ] **Phone/Tablet**
  - Record serial number
  - Remove organization accounts from device
  - Factory reset if it was an organization device

- [ ] **Monitor, keyboard, mouse**
  - If organization-owned

- [ ] **Chargers and cables**

- [ ] **External hard drives or USB drives**

- [ ] **Organization credit cards or purchasing cards**

---

### 5. Data & Files (15 minutes)

**Ensure work product stays with the organization:**

- [ ] **Move files from personal accounts to organization storage**
  - Files in personal Google Drive
  - Files on personal Dropbox
  - Documents on their computer

- [ ] **Document where important files are located**
  - Project files
  - Contact lists
  - Process documentation

- [ ] **Transfer ownership of ongoing projects**
  - Reassign tasks in project management tools
  - Update project documentation with new contact person

- [ ] **Archive or delete personal files from organization systems**
  - Personal photos
  - Personal documents
  - (Be respectful — give them time to save personal items)

---

### 6. Final Check (5 minutes)

**Quick verification:**

- [ ] Try logging in with their credentials (should fail)
- [ ] Check that they're not in any shared password vaults
- [ ] Verify email forwarding is working
- [ ] Confirm all equipment is returned

---

## After They Leave

### 7. Communication (5 minutes)

- [ ] **Notify the team**
  - "[Name] is no longer with us. [New person] is taking over [responsibilities]."
  - Don't need to explain why unless appropriate

- [ ] **Update website**
  - Remove from staff page
  - Remove from contact forms

- [ ] **Update external contacts if necessary**
  - Donors who worked directly with them
  - Partner organizations
  - Vendors

- [ ] **Update emergency contact list**

---

## Sample Offboarding Email

Send this to your IT person or whoever handles access:

```
Subject: Access Removal Required - [Volunteer Name]

Hi [IT Person],

[Volunteer Name] is leaving the organization. Their last day is [Date].

Please remove access to:

EMAIL & ACCOUNTS:
- Email: [name@yourorg.org] - suspend and forward to [supervisor@yourorg.org]
- Google Workspace
- Remove from all distribution lists

PASSWORD ACCESS:
- Remove from Bitwarden Organization
- Change WiFi password (they knew it)

SYSTEMS:
- [Donor database]
- [Social media - list which ones]
- [Project management tool]
- [File storage]

EQUIPMENT TO COLLECT:
- Laptop (Serial: ____)
- Phone (if applicable)
- Keys and access card

Please confirm when complete.

Thanks,
[Your Name]
```

## Special Situations

### Contentious Departures

If someone is being asked to leave or the departure is not amicable:

**Do immediately:**
- Remove all access during the exit conversation or before they leave the building
- Don't wait until "end of day"
- Have someone with you when you collect equipment
- Change all shared passwords they knew

### Volunteers Who Leave Abruptly

If someone stops showing up or doesn't give notice:

1. Attempt to contact them
2. After 48 hours, suspend (don't delete) their accounts
3. Change shared passwords as a precaution
4. If no response after a week, consider it a permanent departure and complete offboarding

### Remote Volunteers

For volunteers who work remotely:

1. Schedule a video call for the exit conversation
2. Send a prepaid shipping label for equipment return
3. Use remote wipe if the device supports it
4. Don't delete their accounts until equipment is returned or confirmed lost

---

## Common Mistakes to Avoid

❌ **Don't:** Wait until "later" to remove access
✅ **Do:** Remove access immediately on their last day

❌ **Don't:** Delete email accounts immediately
✅ **Do:** Suspend and forward for 30 days first

❌ **Don't:** Forget about social media access
✅ **Do:** Check all platforms, including ones you rarely use

❌ **Don't:** Assume they'll return equipment voluntarily
✅ **Do:** Have a clear checklist and get signatures

❌ **Don't:** Forget to tell the team
✅ **Do:** Communicate clearly about the departure and handoffs

❌ **Don't:** Skip documentation
✅ **Do:** Complete the offboarding record for every departure
