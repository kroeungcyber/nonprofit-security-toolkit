# 🔐 Password Manager Setup

**Get Bitwarden working for your non-profit in under an hour.**

## Why This Matters

**The Problem:** Most people use the same password everywhere, or simple passwords that are easy to guess. When one website gets hacked, attackers try that password on email, banking, and work accounts.

**The Solution:** A password manager creates and remembers strong, unique passwords for every account. You only need to remember one master password.

**Real Impact:** Organizations using password managers are 80% less likely to experience account takeovers.

## What is a Password Manager?

Think of it as a secure digital vault that:
- **Remembers all your passwords** — so you don't have to
- **Creates strong passwords** — random 20+ character codes that are impossible to guess
- **Fills in passwords automatically** — click a button, you're logged in
- **Syncs across devices** — works on your computer, phone, and tablet
- **Protects with encryption** — even Bitwarden can't see your passwords

**Analogy:** It's like having a secure safe in your home. You have one key (your master password) that opens the safe, and inside are all your other keys (passwords to websites).

## Step 1: Create Your Organization's Bitwarden Account (15 minutes)

### 1.1 Sign Up

1. Go to [vault.bitwarden.com](https://vault.bitwarden.com)
2. Click **"Create Account"**
3. Enter your email (use your work email)
4. Create your **master password**

### 1.2 Create a Strong Master Password

This is the ONE password you must remember. If you forget it, you lose everything.

**Requirements:**
- At least 12 characters (16+ recommended)
- Mix of uppercase, lowercase, numbers, symbols
- Not based on personal information (birthdays, pet names)
- Memorable but not guessable

**Good examples:**
- `Blue-Monkey-42-Jumps!`
- `MyDog$leepsOnCouch2024`
- `Pizza@Friday#Night9pm`

**Write this down and store it somewhere safe:**
- In a locked drawer
- In a safe
- With your other important documents
- NOT just on your computer or phone

### 1.3 Verify Your Email

1. Check your email for a message from Bitwarden
2. Click the verification link
3. Your account is now active

## Step 2: Install Bitwarden on Your Computer (15 minutes)

### Windows

1. Go to [bitwarden.com/download](https://bitwarden.com/download)
2. Download the Windows installer
3. Run the file (it will be named something like `Bitwarden-Installer.exe`)
4. Follow the installation prompts
5. Open Bitwarden from your Start Menu
6. Log in with your email and master password

### Mac

1. Go to [bitwarden.com/download](https://bitwarden.com/download)
2. Download the Mac app
3. Open the downloaded `.dmg` file
4. Drag Bitwarden to your Applications folder
5. Open Bitwarden from Applications
6. Log in with your email and master password

**Note:** Mac may warn you that Bitwarden is from the internet. This is normal. Click "Open" to continue.

### Install the Browser Extension

The browser extension makes logging into websites automatic.

**Chrome or Edge:**
1. Go to [chrome.google.com/webstore](https://chrome.google.com/webstore)
2. Search "Bitwarden Password Manager"
3. Click "Add to Chrome" (or Edge)
4. Click "Add Extension"
5. Click the Bitwarden icon in your toolbar
6. Log in with your email and master password

**Firefox:**
1. Go to [addons.mozilla.org](https://addons.mozilla.org)
2. Search "Bitwarden"
3. Click "Add to Firefox"
4. Click "Add"
5. Log in when prompted

**Safari (Mac):**
1. Open Safari
2. Go to Safari > Preferences > Extensions
3. Click "Get Extensions"
4. Search "Bitwarden"
5. Install and enable it

## Step 4: Migrate Your Passwords (30 minutes)

Now move your existing passwords into Bitwarden.

### Method 1: Add Passwords One by One (Recommended for small organizations)

**For each important account:**

1. Go to the website and log in normally
2. Change your password to a new strong one:
   - In Bitwarden, click the **+** (Add Item) button
   - Name: Enter the website name (e.g., "Gmail")
   - Username: Your email or username
   - Password: Click the **Generate** icon (circular arrows)
     - Set length to 20
     - Click "Select"
   - Click "Save"
3. Copy the new password from Bitwarden
4. On the website, go to "Change Password" or "Security Settings"
5. Paste the new Bitwarden password
6. Save the changes

**Priority order:**
1. Email accounts (Gmail, Outlook) — MOST IMPORTANT
2. Banking and financial accounts
3. Donor database or CRM
4. Social media accounts
5. Any other work-related accounts

**Tip:** Don't try to do all at once. Do 5-10 per day.

### Method 2: Import from Browser (Faster, but needs cleanup)

If you have passwords saved in Chrome, Firefox, or Safari:

1. **Export from your browser:**
   - Chrome: Settings > Passwords > Export passwords
   - Firefox: about:logins > Export
   - Safari: File > Export > Passwords

2. **Import to Bitwarden:**
   - Go to [vault.bitwarden.com](https://vault.bitwarden.com)
   - Log in
   - Go to **Tools** > **Import Data**
   - Select your browser
   - Upload the file you exported
   - Click "Import Data"

3. **Delete the export file** — it contains unencrypted passwords
4. **Review imported passwords** — delete any old or unused ones

## Step 5: Set Up Organization Sharing (20 minutes)

Some passwords need to be shared (social media accounts, shared email addresses, software licenses).

### Option A: Share Individual Items (Free)

1. In Bitwarden, find the password you want to share
2. Click on it to open
3. Click the **Share** icon
4. Enter the email of the person you're sharing with
5. Choose permission level:
   - **Can View** — they can see and use the password
   - **Can Edit** — they can change the password
6. Click "Save"

### Option B: Create an Organization (Recommended for teams)

An Organization lets you create shared collections of passwords.

1. Go to [vault.bitwarden.com](https://vault.bitwarden.com)
2. Click **"New Organization"** at the top
3. Name it (e.g., "Animal Rescue Team")
4. Choose the free plan
5. Click "Submit"

**Create collections:**
1. In your Organization, click **"Collections"**
2. Click **"New Collection"**
3. Name it (e.g., "Social Media", "Financial", "Software Licenses")
4. Add passwords to the collection

**Invite team members:**
1. Go to **"Manage"** > **"People"**
2. Click **"Invite"**
3. Enter their email
4. Choose their access level:
   - **User** — can use passwords in assigned collections
   - **Manager** — can add/edit passwords in assigned collections
   - **Admin** — full control
5. Assign them to collections
6. Click "Save"

## How to Use Bitwarden Daily

### Logging into Websites

**With browser extension:**
1. Go to a website (e.g., gmail.com)
2. Click the Bitwarden icon in your toolbar
3. Click the account you want to use
4. Bitwarden fills in the username and password automatically

**Without browser extension:**
1. Go to the website
2. Click in the username field
3. Open Bitwarden app
4. Find the account
5. Click the copy icon next to username, paste it
6. Click the copy icon next to password, paste it

### Creating New Accounts

1. Go to the "Sign Up" page
2. Fill in your information
3. For the password field:
   - Open Bitwarden
   - Click **Generator**
   - Set length to 20
   - Click "Copy Password"
   - Paste it into the website
4. Save the new login to Bitwarden immediately

### Generating Strong Passwords

1. Open Bitwarden
2. Go to **Generator**
3. Choose options:
   - **Length:** 20 characters
   - **A-Z:** Yes
   - **a-z:** Yes
   - **0-9:** Yes
   - **!@#$%^&***:** Yes
4. Click "Copy Password"
5. Paste where needed

## Training Your Team

### Quick Training Script (5 minutes per person)

**Show them:**
1. How to log into Bitwarden
2. How to use the browser extension to fill passwords
3. How to generate a new strong password
4. Where to find the master password if they forget (they should have written it down!)

**Give them:**
- This guide printed out
- Permission to ask questions
- A week to transition their most-used passwords

### Common Volunteer Questions

**"Why can't I just use my regular passwords?"**
> "Because if one website gets hacked, attackers can access your email, bank, and work accounts. This keeps everything separate and secure."

**"What if Bitwarden gets hacked?"**
> "Your passwords are encrypted. Even if hackers got in, they'd see scrambled data that they can't read without your master password."

**"This seems complicated."**
> "It takes a few days to get used to, but then it's easier than remembering passwords. I'll help you through the first week."

## Troubleshooting

**"I forgot my master password"**
- Bitwarden cannot reset this for you
- Check where you wrote it down
- If you truly can't remember, you'll need to create a new account and reset all your passwords

**"Bitwarden won't auto-fill"**
- Make sure the browser extension is installed and you're logged in
- Click the Bitwarden icon and select the account manually
- Some websites block auto-fill for security — copy/paste instead

**"The generated password is too long for this website"**
- Some old websites have limits
- Change the generator to 16 or 12 characters
- Most modern sites accept 20+ characters

**"I shared a password but the person can't see it"**
- They need to accept the invitation
- They need to create their own Bitwarden account
- Check that you used the correct email address
