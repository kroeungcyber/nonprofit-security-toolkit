# 👥 Protect Your People

**Securing your team's accounts and managing who has access to what.**

---

## Why This Matters

Most security breaches start with stolen passwords. When attackers get into one account, they often use it to access others. By securing how your team logs in and manages passwords, you stop most attacks before they start.

**What you're protecting:**
- Donor and volunteer personal information
- Financial accounts and banking access
- Email communications
- Your organization's reputation

**Time to implement:** 3-4 hours total  
**Cost:** $0  
**Ongoing maintenance:** 15 minutes per new volunteer

## Common Questions

**"What if someone forgets their Bitwarden master password?"**
- They lose access to all stored passwords
- This is why we emphasize writing it down and storing it safely
- Organization accounts should have recovery options set up

**"Do I need to buy Bitwarden Premium?"**
- No. The free version has everything most non-profits need
- Premium adds features like encrypted file storage and emergency access
- Consider Premium ($10/year) only if you need those extras

**"What about shared accounts like info@ourorg.org?"**
- Store these in Bitwarden and share access
- Better yet: give each person their own login if the service allows it
- If you must share, use the password manager's sharing feature

**"My volunteers aren't tech-savvy. Will they struggle?"**
- Bitwarden is designed for non-technical users
- Plan 30 minutes of training per person
- Create a simple cheat sheet they can reference
- Most people find it easier than remembering multiple passwords
