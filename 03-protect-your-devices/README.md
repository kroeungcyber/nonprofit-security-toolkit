# 💻 Protect Your Devices

**Secure laptops, phones, and tablets against theft, malware, and unauthorized access.**

---

## Why This Matters

Your devices hold the keys to your organization:
- Email accounts
- Donor databases
- Financial records
- Confidential communications

**If a device is lost, stolen, or infected:**
- Someone could access all your data
- Donor information could be exposed
- Your accounts could be compromised
- You could lose important files

**Real story:** A volunteer left their laptop in a coffee shop. It had their email logged in with "remember password" enabled. The person who found it accessed their email, reset passwords for other accounts, and transferred $3,000 from the organization's PayPal before anyone noticed.

---

## What You'll Accomplish

- ✅ Set up automatic security updates
- ✅ Enable built-in antivirus and firewall
- ✅ Configure screen locks and encryption
- ✅ Prepare for lost or stolen devices

**Time to implement:** 2-3 hours  
**Cost:** $0 (using built-in tools)  
**Ongoing maintenance:** 15 minutes per month

**For any device:**

3. ✅ Enable screen lock after 5 minutes
4. ✅ Enable "Find My Device"
5. ✅ Turn on built-in antivirus/firewall

## Security Principles for All Devices

### 1. Keep Software Updated

**Why:** Updates fix security holes that hackers exploit.

**What to update:**
- Operating system (Windows, macOS, iOS, Android)
- Web browsers (Chrome, Firefox, Safari)
- Apps and software
- Antivirus definitions

---

### 2. Use Strong Authentication

**Screen lock:**
- Password or PIN required to unlock
- Not "swipe to unlock" or "none"

**Strong passwords:**
- Windows/Mac: At least 8 characters, but 12+ recommended
- Phones: 6-digit PIN minimum, longer is better
- Avoid: birthdays, simple patterns (123456), repeated digits

**Biometrics:**
- Fingerprint or face unlock is convenient AND secure
- Use them in addition to (not instead of) a password/PIN

---

### 3. Encrypt Your Data

**What encryption does:** Scrambles your data so it's unreadable without your password.

**Why it matters:** If your device is stolen, encrypted data is useless to thieves.

**Good news:**
- Windows 10/11 Pro: BitLocker encryption (built-in)
- macOS: FileVault encryption (built-in, usually on by default)
- iOS: Automatically encrypted
- Android: Encrypted by default on modern devices

---

### 4. Prepare for Loss or Theft

**Before it happens:**
- Enable "Find My Device" / "Find My Mac" / "Find My iPhone"
- Record serial numbers
- Enable remote wipe capability
- Regular backups (see [Backup Your Data](../04-backup-your-data/))

**If it happens:**
- Use Find My Device to locate, lock, or erase
- Change passwords for all accounts on the device
- Report to police if stolen
- Contact your insurance

## Device-Specific Guides

### Windows Computers

See: [Windows Laptop Security Guide](./windows-laptop-security.md)

**Key actions:**
- Enable Windows Defender (free, built-in antivirus)
- Turn on automatic updates
- Set up Windows Hello (fingerprint/face login)
- Enable BitLocker encryption
- Configure firewall

---

### Mac Computers

**Key actions:**
- Enable Gatekeeper (blocks untrusted software)
- Turn on automatic updates
- Set up FileVault encryption
- Enable Find My Mac
- Configure firewall

---

### Phones and Tablets

**Key actions:**
- Set a strong PIN/password (not just a pattern)
- Enable biometric unlock (fingerprint/face)
- Turn on Find My iPhone / Find My Device
- Keep iOS/Android updated
- Only install apps from official stores

## Shared Devices

Some non-profits have shared computers that multiple volunteers use.

### Recommendations:

**Create separate user accounts:**
- Each volunteer has their own login
- Don't share passwords
- Log out when finished

**Or use a guest account:**
- Wipes data after each session
- Good for occasional users

**Document shared passwords securely:**
- Store in Bitwarden, not on sticky notes
- Change periodically
- Remove access when volunteers leave

---

## Bring Your Own Device (BYOD)

Many volunteers use personal devices for work.

### Policy Recommendations:

**Required on personal devices used for work:**
- Screen lock enabled
- Automatic updates
- Device encryption
- Find My Device enabled

**Work data on personal devices:**
- Use work email through official apps (Gmail, Outlook)
- Don't download sensitive files to personal devices
- Use cloud storage (Google Drive) rather than local copies

**When volunteers leave:**
- Remove work email accounts from their devices
- Revoke access to work apps
- Wipe work data if possible
